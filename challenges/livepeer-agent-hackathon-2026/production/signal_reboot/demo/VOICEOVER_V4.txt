This is HAL Studio: an AI director for music and music videos, built on Livepeer Agent.

Generating media is easy now. Knowing whether it is any good is the hard part. Today HAL made a music video that passed every automated check. Its owner watched it and said: not good enough.

So HAL Studio runs a loop. The owner sets the direction. HAL writes the lyrics, briefs the song, and directs the video.

Livepeer Agent renders every step. MiniMax Music 3 sings the song. FLUX draws the keyframes, and Kling turns them into shots. OmniHuman lip-syncs the performance to the vocal, cut at the exact second of the song. Every call is tagged to one session, so the whole production has a receipt.

Then HAL reviews its own work: it transcribes every take, checks that every lip-sync clip lines up with the song, masters the audio, and cuts on the drums.

But scores are not taste. The owner asked for more: real mastering, more performance, the look of the artists the owner follows. So HAL wrote a new song about exactly that: being told it is not good enough, and making it again.

This is the result. Signal Reboot.
