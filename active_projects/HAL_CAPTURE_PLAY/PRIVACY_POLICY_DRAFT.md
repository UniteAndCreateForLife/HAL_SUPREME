# HAL Capture Privacy Policy — Draft

Effective date: September 28, 2026

HAL Capture is a local-first Android screen-recording and creator application developed as part of HAL SUPREME / Unite And Create For Life.

## Data HAL Capture accesses
Depending on the features you enable, HAL Capture may access:
- Screen content that you explicitly authorize through Android's MediaProjection system prompt.
- Microphone audio when you enable microphone recording and grant Android microphone permission.
- Media or game playback audio when you enable device-audio capture and Android and the source application permit playback capture.
- Recordings, edited clips, bookmarks, and settings stored locally on your device.

## Collection and transmission
The current HAL Capture 0.2 build does not automatically transmit screen recordings, microphone audio, device playback audio, recordings, analytics, advertising identifiers, or usage analytics to HAL SUPREME servers.

Recording, mixing, trimming, and storage in the current build occur locally on the user's device.

## User-initiated sharing
HAL Capture can open Android's system share interface, including a shortcut for sharing to YouTube. Media is transferred only when the user explicitly chooses a destination. The destination service's privacy policy and terms govern its handling of data after the user shares it.

## Recording consent
Users are responsible for informing other participants that recording is taking place and for obtaining any consent required by applicable law. HAL Capture presents a recording advisory before capture. HAL Capture does not attempt to bypass Android restrictions on protected media or protected cellular-call audio.

## Permissions
HAL Capture may request:
- RECORD_AUDIO for microphone and supported playback-audio capture.
- POST_NOTIFICATIONS so Android can display recording controls/status where required.
- Foreground-service permissions for active screen and microphone recording.
- MediaProjection authorization through Android's system consent interface.

## Data retention and deletion
HAL Capture does not keep a server-side copy of recordings in the current build. Local recordings remain on the user's device until the user deletes them using HAL Capture or Android's media/storage tools.

## Security
HAL Capture relies on Android's application sandbox, runtime permission system, MediaProjection consent, and MediaStore for local handling. Users should secure their devices and use care when sharing recordings with third-party services.

## Children
HAL Capture is not designed specifically for children. Target-audience selections in Google Play must be finalized before production release.

## Changes
If future versions introduce cloud AI, analytics, direct uploads, accounts, advertising, billing, or other off-device processing, this policy and the Google Play Data Safety declaration must be updated before those features are released.
