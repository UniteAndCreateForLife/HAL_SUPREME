# HAL Capture Studio — Play Store Listing Draft

## App name
HAL Capture Studio

## Short description
Record your screen and audio, trim clips, and share videos from your Android phone.

## Full description
HAL Capture Studio is a local-first Android screen recorder and creator tool.

Capture your screen while using other apps, record microphone audio, capture supported media/game playback audio, pause and resume from the recording notification, add bookmarks, then save the finished MP4 to your device.

The current release includes:
- Background screen recording using Android MediaProjection
- Screen-only, microphone, device-audio, or device-audio + microphone modes
- Local audio mixing and AAC encoding
- Pause/resume, bookmarks, stop and save
- Local MP4 storage under Movies/HAL Capture
- Quick lossless trimming
- Android share flow with a YouTube share shortcut
- Clear recording and consent disclosures
- No analytics and no automatic cloud upload

Important platform note: Android and source apps control whether playback audio may be captured. Protected content and apps that opt out of playback capture are not bypassed. Cellular-call remote audio is not universally available to normal third-party Android apps.

HAL Capture is being developed as part of the HAL SUPREME creator-tool ecosystem.
