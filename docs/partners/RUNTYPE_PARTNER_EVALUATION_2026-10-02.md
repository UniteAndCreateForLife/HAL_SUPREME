## Fit

Runtype's current public Solution Partner program describes:
- Claude + Runtype MCP workflows;
- product evals and regression checks;
- per-client usage/spend controls;
- protected parameters;
- managed prototyping with graduation to AWS, GCP, or Vercel;
- Solution, Technology, and Referral partner tracks.

The public page says users can start building free. It also describes a Team plan starting at $400/month with onboarding and training.
